Master’s Thesis

Optimization of an Energy Management System in a Hybrid Microgrid

USTO-MB, Oran · 2025 — designed and simulated an energy management system for a small-scale hybrid microgrid integrating photovoltaic panels, wind turbines, battery storage and electric vehicles.

01

Problem

A hybrid microgrid draws from intermittent sources — sun and wind — while serving a demand curve that does not match generation. Batteries and electric vehicles add storage that can charge or discharge, so supply, demand and cost have to be balanced continuously rather than sized once.

02

Approach

I modelled the full system in MATLAB and Simulink — PV array, wind turbine, battery bank and EV charging — then developed optimization algorithms that dispatch each source against load and cost constraints across the operating cycle.

03

Outcome

The simulated management strategy improved system efficiency and operational reliability, keeping the load served while reducing cost and easing stress on storage. The work formed the basis of my Master’s degree, awarded in 2025.
